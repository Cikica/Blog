title: "You need to be reminded more than you need to be taught"
---

Knowledge is all the understanding accumulated throughout your life experience [so far].
* It can be Special or Mundane.
* It can be Conscious or Unconscious.
* The amount of knowledge we can amass throughout our life is tiny compared to the total knowledge of our society, [let alone the unknown knowledge we have yet to discover].
* Having knowledge != being good at explaining it.

**So what do you do with this?**
* You will always be a specialist
* You will always be in a position in which you must trust the expertise of others, in fact our whole society is predicate on it.


**Properties of Knowledge**
* Knowledge can be Special.
* Knowledge can be Mundane.
* Each of which can be split into a) Conscious, and b) Unconscious types.
* Your ability to amass knowledge is limited.
* You ability to pass on knowledge is separate from the ability knowledge gives you.
