order: "1"
---

The amount of things needed to learn in order to live a fulfilling and good life are small.

It is the constant practice, the getting back on the path after you have wandered off it, that is the challenge.

We should practice the things that make us the people we want to be. 

We should practice the things that take us where we say we want to go.

We should practice until they are first nature.
