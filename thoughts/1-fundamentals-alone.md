title: "You can get further than you can possibly imagine on fundamentals alone."
excerpt: "Perhaps further than on anything else."
order: "1"
---

Perhaps further than on anything else.

This seems to be true in nearly every discipline, but I'm sure there are exceptions.

Learning and mastering fundamentals when picking up a new skill seems to skyrocket your ability quickly, whilst keeping complexity of learning low.

**I could swear that fundamentals are the pareto of their discipline?** (in terms of skill gained per unit of learning effort).

So if I wanted to be above average in a skill with least amount of possible effort, just mastering the fundamentals seems to be the pathway.
